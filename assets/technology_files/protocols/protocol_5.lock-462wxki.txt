PROTOCOL V-05 — AТРSYNTHASE GRADIENT CONFIRMATION AND DIVS CHECKPOINT TUNING
Target: Proton gradient integrity + DivS-gated fisZ threshold for cell division
Version: 1.9
Last revised: cycle 35, day 29

================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Confirm that the electron transport chain is generating a proton-motive force
(PMF) across the inner membrane and that ATP synthase is coupling this gradient
to ATP production. Then tune the DivS checkpoint — the molecular gatekeeper
that requires both high ATP: ADP and a GROW-state toggle before releasing the
fisZ division trigger.

NEW REAGENTS
------------
DCCD (dicyclohexylcarbodiimide): ATP synthase inhibitor, 200 mM stock in
  ethanol, working concentration 50 µM. Used to confirm ATP production is
  gradient-dependent. CAUTION: DCCD is poorly soluble in water — add to
  medium while stirring; vortex the ethanol stock before using. If you add
  it without stirring, it precipitates on your cells immediately, and the
  inhibition is inconsistent.
CCCP (carbonyl cyanide m-chlorophenylhydrazone): protonophore, 5 mM stock
  in ethanol, working concentration 20 µM. Used to collapse the PMF as a
  control. Also affects mitochondria, in case anyone is using this protocol
  in a eukaryotic system for some reason. You are not; you are using VB-Δsid.
ATP/ADP Bioluminescence Assay Kit (commercial, e.g. Sigma-Aldrich MAK135 or
  equivalent). Store at -80°C. Follow kit protocol exactly for calibration —
  the standard curve must be generated fresh each assay day, not reused from
  a previous day. The luciferin in the kit degrades. Old standard curves give
  false-low ATP readings and make your threshold measurements useless.
fisZ synthetic gene: 1.1 kb, encodes the cell-division ring protein. This gene
  is placed under DivS checkpoint control — DivS protein (itself constitutively
  expressed) only allows fisZ transcription when Fe²⁺: Fe³⁺ toggle is in GROW
  state AND ATP: ADP is above threshold.

PROCEDURE
---------

Step 1 — Confirm proton gradient formation (Day 1-2)

Grow full metabolic VB-Δsid strain (carrying V-01, V-02, V-03, V-04 constructs)
anaerobically on mineral substrate, as per V-03.

At OD₆₀₀ 0.5, split into three flasks:
  Flask A: untreated control
  Flask B: add CCCP to 20 µM (collapses PMF)
  Flask C: add DCCD to 50 µM (blocks ATP synthase but preserves PMF)

Incubate for 30 min at 30°C, anaerobically.

Measure ATP and ADP in all three flasks using the bioluminescence kit.
Sample 50 µL per time point, quench immediately in 50 µL of the kit's lysis
reagent (pre-warmed to 30°C — the reagent must be warm; cold lysis reagent
slows the reaction and underestimates ATP). Run assay per kit protocol.

Expected:
  Flask A: ATP:ADP ratio 4-8 (healthy, energy-replete cells)
  Flask B: ATP:ADP drops to <0.5 within 15 min (no gradient, no ATP synthesis)
  Flask C: ATP:ADP rises transiently then crashes (PMF intact, ATP synthase
    blocked, cells burn through ATP reserves without replenishing them)

This confirms the cells ARE making ATP via the proton gradient. If Flask B
shows no change: either CCCP is inactive (check ethanol stock age, CCCP
degrades over months) or the cells have another ATP source you haven't
accounted for. Check for substrate-level phosphorylation in the carbon source
— M9 + glucose can sustain glycolysis independent of the ETC.

Step 2 — Clone DivS checkpoint construct (Day 1-4, parallel)

DivS acts as a two-input AND gate:
  Input 1: Toggle in GROW state (divR protein produced, which releases
    DivS from an autoinhibited complex — exact mechanism in V-05-mech-notes)
  Input 2: ATP:ADP above threshold (DivS has an internal ATP-binding domain
    that holds it inactive at low ATP — when ATP is abundant, the domain
    changes conformation and releases the fisZ promoter)

Clone DivS and fisZ:
  P_const-W — divS (constitutive, always expressed)
  DivS-binding element — P_fisZ — fisZ

The DivS-binding element upstream of P_fisZ is the tunable part. We have three
variants corresponding to different ATP binding affinities:
  DivS-BE-Lo: low ATP threshold (Kd ~0.5 mM ATP) — fast division, low security
  DivS-BE-Mid: medium threshold (Kd ~2 mM ATP) — balanced (wild-type default)
  DivS-BE-Hi: high threshold (Kd ~8 mM ATP) — slow division, high energy reserve

ATP concentrations in bacteria typically range 1-10 mM in healthy cells.
DivS-BE-Mid (Kd 2 mM) means: cells need to be roughly at 50% max energetic
capacity before being allowed to divide — reasonable, sensible, middle of
the spectrum. Matches r/K ecological middle ground.

Proceed with cloning as per V-01 workflow. Use pBBR1-origin vector (third
distinct origin if carrying V-01 and V-02 constructs). Verify by sequencing.

Step 3 — Validate DivS threshold tuning (Day 5-10)

Transform DivS-BE-Mid construct into full metabolic strain.
Grow anaerobically on mineral slurry, track both ATP: ADP ratio (kit, Step 1)
and division rate (OD₆₀₀ doubling time AND microscopy — the VB cells are
rod-shaped, 2-3 µm, division creates symmetric daughter pairs, count them).

Manipulate ATP availability by varying electron flux (mineral concentration,
as in V-04 Step 4). Low mineral = low electron flux = low ATP = DivS stuck =
no fisZ = no division. High mineral = high electron flux = high ATP = DivS
released = fisZ expressed = division proceeds.

The ATP: ADP threshold for division onset should match the DivS-BE binding Kd.
If division starts at much lower ATP than predicted: DivS is leaking (check
construct — there may be a secondary promoter upstream of fisZ in your vector
backbone that bypasses the checkpoint entirely. This happened to me in version
1.3 and I didn't catch it for three experiments. Map your vector carefully.)

If division never starts despite high ATP: DivS may not be sensing the toggle
correctly. Toggle confirmation first (V-02, Step 6), then come back to this.
Do not optimize the checkpoint before confirming the toggle is working.
The temptation to skip ahead is strong. Resist it.
