PROTOCOL V-02 — CONSTRUCTION AND VALIDATION OF THE BISTABLE TOGGLE SWITCH
Target: growR / divR mutual repression circuit
Version: 4.1
Last revised: cycle 34, day 22

================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Construct and tune the bistable genetic toggle: growR under P_scav, divR under
P_grow, each repressor shutting the other's promoter. Wire volR (Fe³⁺-
dependent, from V-01 constructs) as an additional repressor on P_scav to
create the iron-gated flip signal. Validate bistability by demonstrating
hysteresis — the switch stays committed to its last state even after the
triggering signal is removed.

MATERIALS LIST
--------------
Plasmid backbones (TWO separate vectors required — critical):
  pVB-KAN02: used for P_scav — growR cassette (KanR selection)
  pVB-CHL01: used for P_grow — divR cassette (ChloramR selection)
  ** Both vectors MUST have different origins of replication. pVB-KAN02 uses
  pUC origin (high copy). pVB-CHL01 uses p15A origin (medium copy). If you
  try to use two pUC-origin plasmids together, they will compete for
  replication, and one will be lost within 5-10 generations.  

Synthetic gene blocks:
  - growR: 900 bp, encodes the growth-phase repressor — resuspend to 100 ng/µL
  - divR: 780 bp, encodes the division-phase repressor — resuspend to 100 ng/µL
  - volR: 1.1 kb, encodes Fur-family Fe³⁺ sensor — resuspend to 100 ng/µL
  - P_scav promoter cassette with volR binding site incorporated
  - P_grow promoter cassette: 380 bp — resuspend to 50 ng/µL

Purified repressor proteins for in vitro tuning (Steps 4-5):
  - GrowR-His₆ recombinant protein, purified from E. coli expression strain,
    stored in storage buffer (50 mM Tris pH 7.5, 150 mM NaCl, 10% glycerol,
    1 mM DTT) at -80°C — 200 µL aliquots at 5 µM
  - DivR-His₆ recombinant protein, same storage buffer, 200 µL at 5 µM
  - Preparation notes in separate file V-02-protein-prep.txt (ask me for it,
    do not attempt to re-express these yourself without that document)

Binding site sequences for tuning (see Step 5):
  The strength of mutual repression is set by how well GrowR binds to P_grow
  and DivR binds to P_scav. We have three binding site variants for each:
  Variant A (tight, Kd ~1 nM) — stubborn switch, hard to flip
  Variant B (medium, Kd ~10 nM) — balanced switch
  Variant C (loose, Kd ~100 nM) — twitchy switch, flips on a breeze
  Binding site sequences are in blue binder, tab V-02-BS.

EMSA buffer (for gel-shift assays in Step 5):
  20 mM HEPES pH 7.4, 100 mM KCl, 5 mM MgCl₂, 0.1 mM EDTA, 1 mM DTT,
  5% glycerol — make fresh, filter sterile. DTT is added last from 1 M stock.

EMSA gel: 6% native PAGE in 0.5× TBE, pre-run at 100V for 30 min before
  loading to clear ionic contaminants from the gel matrix.

FeCl₃ stock: 100 mM in 10 mM HCl (to prevent hydrolysis), filter-sterile.
  Working concentration for toggle-flip experiments: 500 µM added to medium
  (final). This is the Fe³⁺ pulse that flips the switch toward growth state.
  CAUTION: above 1 mM, Fe³⁺ begins to precipitate in most buffers as Fe(OH)₃.
  Your medium will turn brown, and your experiment is ruined. Stay at 500 µM.

PROCEDURE
---------

Step 1 — Construct P_scav–growR cassette in pVB-KAN02 (Day 1-3)
Follow the same restriction-digest and Gibson assembly approach from V-01,
Steps 1-4. Assemble P_scav (with volR binding site) upstream of growR.
Correct insert size: ~1.35 kb. Clone into pVB-KAN02.
Verify by diagnostic digest and sequencing as in V-01, Step 6.

Step 2 — Construct P_grow–divR cassette in pVB-CHL01 (Day 1-3, parallel)
Assemble P_grow upstream of divR. Correct insert size: ~1.16 kb.
Clone into pVB-CHL01.
Verify by diagnostic digest and sequencing.
Note: chloramphenicol selection plates use 34 µg/mL — NOT the same as the
kanamycin concentration from V-01. Label your plates carefully.
I have a system: yellow dot = Kan, green dot = Chl. The dots are in the
drawer next to the pH meter.

Step 3 — Co-transformation of both constructs (Day 4)

CRITICAL STEP. Both plasmids must go into the same cell simultaneously.
Do NOT transform sequentially (one, grow up, transform the second). By the
time you do the second transformation, the first plasmid may already be
silencing or titrating transcription factors in ways that skew the second
plasmid's expression before the circuit is even assembled.

Combine 2 µL of each confirmed construct miniprep (~100 ng each total DNA).
Use VB-Δsid competent cells as before. Proceed with chemical transformation
protocol from V-01, Step 5 exactly. Recovery in SOC for 1 hour.

Plate on LB agar containing BOTH kanamycin (50 µg/mL) AND chloramphenicol
(34 µg/mL) simultaneously. Only cells carrying both plasmids will grow.
Incubate at 30°C (remember: not 37°C for VB-Δsid), 48-60 hrs.

Expect fewer colonies than single-plasmid transformations — dual selection is
harsh. If you see zero colonies, drop to 25 µg/mL Kan and 17 µg/mL Chl for
the rescue plate. Mark those as "reduced selection" in your notebook.

Step 4 — Verify circuit connectivity (Day 5-6)

Grow 3 confirmed double-transformants to mid-log (OD₆₀₀ 0.4-0.6) in
LB + dual antibiotics, 30°C, 200 rpm.

Pulse with FeCl₃ to 500 µM final. Collect samples at 0, 15, 30, 60, 120 min
post-pulse. Freeze immediately in liquid nitrogen. Store at -80°C.

Extract RNA using a bead-beating protocol (the VB outer membrane resists
standard column lysis — bead-beat 2× 30 sec at 6.5 m/s in RLT buffer,
with 0.1 mm silica beads, then proceed with spin-column cleanup).

Quantify volA, growR, and divR transcript levels by RT-qPCR using primers in
the database (V-02-qPCR-primers). Normalize to rrsA (16S rRNA), not to gyrB —
gyrB expression is iron-sensitive in this strain.

Expected result: volA and growR should FALL after Fe³⁺ pulse (P_scav repressed
by volR-Fe³⁺). divR should RISE (P_grow de-repressed as growR falls).
This confirms the flip direction.

Step 5 — Binding site tuning by EMSA (Day 7-10)

In vitro test of repressor-promoter affinities before committing to a variant.
Run separate gel-shift assays for:
  (a) GrowR-His₆ protein binding to P_grow variants A, B, C
  (b) DivR-His₆ protein binding to P_scav variants A, B, C

For each EMSA reaction (15 µL):
  - 2 µL EMSA buffer 5×
  - 50 nM fluorescently labeled promoter fragment (order from synthesis,
    5' 6-FAM labeled on top strand, ~380 bp, HPLC-purified)
  - Repressor protein titration: 0, 0.5, 1, 5, 10, 50, 100, 500 nM
    (prepare serial dilutions in EMSA buffer from 5 µM stock, use 1:10 steps)
  - Nuclease-free H₂O to 15 µL

Incubate 30 min, room temp, in the DARK (6-FAM photobleaches).
Load directly onto pre-run 6% native PAGE gel without adding loading dye
(loading dye competes with protein binding). Run at 100V, 4°C cold room,
90 minutes.

Image on fluorescence scanner, FAM channel (excitation 488 nm, emission
520 nm filter). The shifted band = promoter: repressor complex. Unshifted band
= free DNA. Kd is approximately the protein concentration at which ~50% of DNA
is shifted.

Pick binding site variants based on your design goal:
  Tight sites (A) for both = stubborn switch, slow response, resilient
  Loose sites (C) for both = fast switch, sensitive, fragile
  Asymmetric (A for one, C for other) = directional bias in the toggle

Default recommendation for wild-type Voltibacter: Variant B both sides.

Step 6 — Hysteresis validation (Day 11-14)

This is the proof of bistability. The switch must STAY in whichever state it
was last flipped to, even after the iron signal is removed.

Grow confirmed circuit strain overnight. Split culture into two flasks:
  Flask 1 (iron-rich pre-condition): supplement with 500 µM FeCl₃ for 6 hours
    (cells should be in GROW state: low volA, high divR)
  Flask 2 (iron-poor pre-condition): add 100 µM 2,2'-dipyridyl for 6 hours
    (cells should be in SCAVENGE state: high volA, low divR)

Then switch both flasks to NEUTRAL iron (M9 + 10 µM FeCl₃, which is below
the toggle-flip threshold but above starvation — the signal is gone, but
the cells are not stressed). Grow for 4 more hours.

Sample both flasks every 30 minutes. Measure volA and divR transcript by
qPCR as in Step 4.

A bistable switch will show: Flask 1 stays high divR / low volA even in
neutral iron. Flask 2 stays high volA / low divR even in neutral iron.
A non-bistable (purely responsive) circuit will converge to the same
intermediate expression level in both flasks by the end of 4 hours.

If your circuit is not bistable: your repressor binding sites are too weak
(too much leaky transcription bleeds through and erodes commitment). Go back
to Step 5 and try tighter variants.

If your circuit IS bistable: write it down. Make a backup of the construct.
