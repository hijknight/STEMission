PROTOCOL V-04 — BIOLUMINESCENCE OVERFLOW VALVE: CLONING AND CALIBRATION
Target: luciferase-family enzyme wired to ETC overflow
Version: 3.0
Last revised: cycle 35, day 14

================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Wire a bacterial luciferase-family enzyme to the electron-transport chain
overflow node so that excess reducing power is harmlessly dissipated as photon
emission rather than damaging the cell via oxidative stress. The glow is a
SAFETY VALVE, not a signaling molecule. Important for accuracy and
important for not electrocuting your chassis from the inside.

ADDITIONAL REAGENTS
-------------------
Luciferase gene module: luxAB-V (a version of bacterial luciferase with
  modified substrate affinity tuned for the intracellular redox state of VB —
  see synthesis notes V-04-synth for modification rationale)
Substrate for in vitro validation (Step 3 only):
  Decanal (n-decyl aldehyde): light-sensitive, volatile, stored at 4°C sealed,
  DO NOT store near the cold room door; the temperature cycling causes the
  cap to loosen. Use 10 µL of neat decanal added to 1 mL buffer = ~60 mM stock.
  Working concentration for assay: 100 µM final in reaction.
  SEPARATELY: FMNH₂ (reduced riboflavin mononucleotide): 1 mM stock in 100 mM
  phosphate buffer pH 7.0 with 1 mM DTT. Prepare fresh daily. FMNH₂ oxidizes
  rapidly in air — make it in the anaerobic box, transfer in a sealed tube.
  Working concentration: 250 µM in the reaction.

Luminometer or plate reader with luminescence detection mode
  (integration time 1 second minimum, 10 seconds preferred for dim signals)

PROCEDURE
---------

Step 1 — Clone luxAB-V under overflow-responsive promoter (Day 1-4)

The overflow promoter P_overflow is a synthetic construct that responds to
elevated quinol: quinone ratio in the inner membrane — when the ETC is saturated
(too many electrons, nowhere to send them), the quinol pool backs up, and this
activates P_overflow. This promoter was characterized in V-04 preliminary work
(cycle 33 notebook). Clone as: P_overflow — luxA — luxB in that order. luxA
and luxB must be co-expressed and in the correct stoichiometry — LuxA: LuxB
must form an αβ heterodimer. If you express them separately on different
vectors at different copy numbers, the ratio goes wrong.

Proceed with standard cloning (V-01 Steps 1-4) into pVB-CHL01 or pBBR1-KAN03
depending on which vectors are already in use. Remember distinct origins.

Step 2 — Transform and select (Day 4-5)
As V-01, Step 5. Plate on appropriate antibiotics. Verify insert by sequencing.

Step 3 — In vitro luminescence validation (Day 6)

Before testing in living cells, confirm LuxAB-V enzyme activity in cell extract.
Grow confirmed construct to OD₆₀₀ 0.8 in LB + antibiotic. Chill on ice.
Pellet cells (4,000 × g, 10 min, 4°C). Wash once with cold PBS.
Resuspend in 500 µL lysis buffer (V-03 Step 5 recipe). Sonicate as before.
Clarify at 16,000 × g, 15 min, 4°C. Transfer supernatant (crude lysate).
Protein concentration by Bradford assay: aim for 1-2 mg/mL.

Luciferase assay (perform in the DARK or subdued amber light):
In a 1.5 mL Eppendorf:
  - 200 µL crude lysate
  - 50 µL 1 mM FMNH₂ stock (250 µM final)
  - 10 µL 60 mM decanal stock in DMSO (allow 5 min pre-equilibration in DMSO
    before adding to aqueous — it will cloud the solution briefly; that is
    normal; it clears as the aldehyde disperses)
  - Phosphate buffer to 400 µL total

Add decanal LAST. Start the luminometer immediately after mixing.
Peak emission is at ~490 nm, blue-green. The reaction is fast — you have
about 60-90 seconds of strong signal before it drops. Do not pipette during
measurement.

Positive control: use a strain expressing wild-type luxAB (non-modified) as
  signal reference.
Negative control: lysate from cells carrying empty vector — any signal here
  is contamination or equipment noise.

Normalized output: relative light units (RLU) per mg protein.
A functional LuxAB-V preparation should give ≥10,000 RLU/mg in this assay.

Step 4 — In vivo calibration of overflow threshold (Day 7-14)

Wire the construct into the full VB-Δsid background carrying V-01 and V-03
constructs (full metabolic strain). Grow anaerobically on mineral substrate
as per V-03 protocol.

Manipulate electron input rate by varying magnetite concentration (0.1, 0.5,
1.0, 5.0 mg/mL) — higher mineral concentration = more electrons available =
higher risk of ETC saturation = more overflow glow.

Measure luminescence in real-time using the plate reader (luminescence mode,
30°C, no agitation during reads — cells need contact with mineral, see V-03).
Simultaneously measure Fe²⁺ accumulation (Ferrozine assay, V-03 Step 4) to
track electron flux.

The overflow threshold is correct when: luminescence only rises AFTER electron
flux plateaus (ETC saturated). If luminescence rises proportionally to electron
flux from the beginning, the overflow promoter is too sensitive — P_overflow
threshold needs to be recalibrated (contact synthesis team for new variant).
