PROTOCOL V-01 — ASSEMBLY AND TRANSFORMATION OF THE SCAVENGER OPERON
Target: volA / volB / volC under promoter P_scav
Version: 7.2
Last revised: cycle 34, day 9

================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Assemble the three-gene iron-uptake relay (outer membrane porin volA, ABC
transporter volB, ferric reductase volC) as a single operon under the iron-
responsive promoter P_scav, clone into a kanamycin-resistant vector, and
transform into the Voltibacter chassis strain VB-Δsid (siderophore-null
background, ensures any iron uptake we see is actually ours).

EQUIPMENT
---------
- Thermal cycler
- Gel electrophoresis rig, 1% agarose, TAE buffer
- Microcentrifuge
- 42°C water bath (CALIBRATED — use the thermometer taped to the side,
  not the dial)
- Spectrophotometer
- Incubator shaker, 37°C, 200 rpm
- Electroporator OR chemical transformation setup — see Step 7 note

REAGENTS & AMOUNTS
------------------
Synthetic gene blocks (ordered from synthesis supplier, double-stranded,
codon-optimized for VB-Δsid GC content of 62%):
  - volA fragment: 2.3 kb, delivered lyophilized, resuspend in 50 µL nuclease-
    free H₂O to 100 ng/µL, store at -20°C in 5 µL aliquots
  - volB fragment: 3.1 kb, same resuspension, 100 ng/µL
  - volC fragment: 1.7 kb, same resuspension, 100 ng/µL
  - P_scav promoter cassette: 420 bp, 50 ng/µL

Cloning vector: pVB-KAN02 (pUC origin, KanR, multiple cloning site with XbaI /
  SpeI / EcoRI / HindIII sites in that order — NOTE: XbaI and SpeI generate
  compatible overhangs. Do NOT use both in the same ligation.)

Restriction enzymes and 10× buffers (all NEB or equivalent, stored at -20°C):
  - XbaI: 20 U/µL — 1 µL per digest reaction
  - EcoRI-HF: 20 U/µL — 1 µL per digest reaction
  - HindIII-HF: 20 U/µL — 1 µL per digest reaction
  - CutSmart Buffer 10×: 5 µL per 50 µL reaction

T4 DNA Ligase: 400 U/µL — 1 µL per reaction
T4 Ligase Buffer 10× (contains ATP): 2 µL per 20 µL reaction
  !! IMPORTANT: Thaw on ice. The ATP in this buffer degrades at room temp.
  If you left it on the bench again, make fresh. ATP kits are in drawer 4B. !!

Gibson Assembly Master Mix (NEB #E2611): 10 µL per 20 µL reaction, thaw on ice
  (Used for upstream operon junction assembly — see Step 3)

LB broth: 10 g/L tryptone, 5 g/L yeast extract, 10 g/L NaCl, pH 7.0
LB agar plates + 50 µg/mL kanamycin (NOT 100 µg/mL — VB-Δsid is sensitive
  at higher concentrations, kills your transformants, adds 48 hrs to your week)

SOC recovery medium: 20 g/L tryptone, 5 g/L yeast extract, 0.5 g/L NaCl,
  2.5 mM KCl, 10 mM MgCl₂, 20 mM glucose — autoclave, add MgCl₂ and glucose
  after cooling, filter-sterilize

Iron-chelated minimal medium (for functional screening post-transformation):
  M9 salts base (6 g/L Na₂HPO₄, 3 g/L KH₂PO₄, 0.5 g/L NaCl, 1 g/L NH₄Cl),
  supplemented with 0.2% glucose, 2 mM MgSO₄, 0.1 mM CaCl₂, and 100 µM
  2,2'-dipyridyl as an iron chelator — this creates the Fe³⁺-limiting condition
  that should turn P_scav on. Make fresh. 2,2'-dipyridyl is light-sensitive,
  foil-wrap the flask, or your results will be trash and you will be sad.

Competent cells: VB-Δsid chemically competent stock, -80°C. 50 µL aliquots.
  Thaw on ice for 10- 15 min. They are NOT electrocompetent — the outer membrane
  composition of the VB chassis cannot tolerate the pulse. Replacing the electroporator 
  cuvette holder is apparently not covered under warranty.

PROCEDURE
---------

Step 1 — Fragment verification (Day 1, ~2 hrs)
Thaw one aliquot each of volA, volB, volC, P_scav. Run 1 µL of each on a
1% agarose gel (TAE, 100V, 35 min) alongside a 1 kb+ ladder. Confirm band
sizes before touching anything else. 

Expected sizes: volA ~2.3 kb, volB ~3.1 kb, volC ~1.7 kb, P_scav ~420 bp.

Step 2 — Restriction digest of fragments and vector (Day 1, ~2 hrs + overnight)

For each fragment (volA, volB, volC) in individual 0.2 mL PCR tubes:
  - 5 µL fragment DNA (~100 ng total)
  - 5 µL CutSmart 10× buffer
  - 1 µL XbaI
  - 1 µL EcoRI-HF
  - 38 µL nuclease-free H₂O
  Total: 50 µL per reaction

Incubate at 37°C, 2 hours. Heat-inactivate at 80°C for 20 minutes.

For vector pVB-KAN02 linearization:
  - 10 µL vector miniprep (~500 ng total)
  - 5 µL CutSmart 10×
  - 1 µL XbaI
  - 1 µL EcoRI-HF
  - 33 µL nuclease-free H₂O
  Total: 50 µL

Incubate at 37°C for 2 hours. Gel-purify the linearized vector band (cut out the
~4.2 kb band, avoid the small stuffer fragment above it). Use a gel extraction
kit per manufacturer protocol. Elute in 30 µL nuclease-free H₂O.

Nanodrop all purified digests. You want ≥15 ng/µL for fragments, ≥20 ng/µL
for vector. Below that: re-run the digest with fresh enzyme or reorder DNA.

Step 3 — Operon junction assembly via Gibson (Day 2, ~1 hr + overnight gel)

The three fragments need to be stitched in order: P_scav — volA — volB — volC.
Gibson assembly requires 15-30 bp overlaps between adjacent fragments. These
overlaps were designed into the synthesis order — verify by checking the
synthesis report (should be in folder V-01/synth_reports on the drive).

Gibson reaction:
  - 10 µL Gibson Assembly Master Mix (thawed on ice)
  - P_scav: 0.05 pmol (~1.5 ng of the 420 bp fragment — TINY amount, use
    a 1:10 dilution of your stock)
  - volA: 0.05 pmol (~3.5 ng)
  - volB: 0.05 pmol (~4.7 ng) 
  - volC: 0.05 pmol (~2.5 ng)
  - Nuclease-free H₂O to 20 µL total

50°C for exactly 60 minutes in the thermal cycler (lid temp 55°C).
Hold at 4°C after. DO NOT leave at 50°C longer — the exonuclease in the mix
will start chewing into your assembled product. Sixty minutes. Set a timer.

After assembly: run 2 µL on a gel to check for product at ~7.5 kb (P_scav +
all three genes assembled). A faint band is fine — Gibson products are not
abundant. If you see nothing, your overlaps are wrong, or the master mix is old.

Step 4 — Ligation into vector (Day 2, ~15 min setup, overnight incubation)

Ligation reaction (20 µL total):
  - 2 µL T4 Ligase Buffer 10× (thawed on ice, vortexed gently, spin down)
  - 1 µL T4 DNA Ligase (400 U/µL)
  - Vector (gel-purified, linearized): 50 ng (~3 µL of your gel extraction)
  - Gibson product (insert): molar ratio 3:1 insert: vector — for a ~7.5 kb
    insert and ~4.2 kb vector, this works out to ~100 ng insert. Use 5 µL of
    the Gibson reaction directly (don't purify between steps, the buffer is
    compatible).
  - Nuclease-free H₂O to 20 µL

Incubate at 16°C overnight (12-16 hrs). The water bath with the floating foam
rack works for this — NOT the 37°C incubator that's set to 16°C. That one runs
warm and will inactivate the ligase by morning. I put a red dot on the
correct one after version 4.0.

Step 5 — Transformation (Day 3, ~3 hrs)

Remove competent cells from -80°C. Thaw on ice — 10 minutes minimum, 20 max.
They are CHEMICAL competent. Do not put them near the electroporator.

Add 5 µL of ligation reaction directly to the 50 µL cell aliquot.
Flick gently to mix. Do NOT pipette up and down — shear forces kill them.
Incubate on ice: 30 minutes.

Heat shock: 42°C water bath, exactly 90 seconds. Transfer IMMEDIATELY back
to ice. Immediately means now. Not when you finish your coffee.

Add 450 µL room-temp SOC medium. Incubate at 37°C, 200 rpm, 1 hour recovery.

Plate 100 µL and 200 µL volumes on pre-warmed LB+Kan (50 µg/mL) agar plates.
Incubate inverted at 37°C overnight (14-16 hrs).

Step 6 — Colony screening and verification (Day 4)

Pick 8-12 colonies per plate (white tip, gentle drag — these cells are fragile
post-transformation, do not stab the agar like you're angry at it).
Inoculate into 5 mL LB+Kan in 15 mL tubes. Grow at 37°C, 200 rpm, overnight.

Miniprep using standard spin-column kit. Elute in 50 µL nuclease-free H₂O.
Nanodrop: expect 50-200 ng/µL for a healthy miniprep from this strain.

VERIFY BY DIAGNOSTIC DIGEST:
  Single digest with EcoRI-HF on 500 ng miniprep DNA.
  Expected bands: ~4.2 kb (vector) + ~7.5 kb (insert) if construct is correct.
  Any other pattern = wrong. Re-pick colonies.

Send 3-4 confirmed candidates for Sanger sequencing with primers P_scav-F and
volC-R (sequences in the primer database, folder V-primers, blue binder).
Confirm full operon sequence integrity before moving to Step 7.

Step 7 — Functional screening (Day 5-6)

Transform confirmed construct into the VB-Δsid working strain (separate
transformation from the cloning strain — use the VB-Δsid chemically competent
stock labeled "WORKING" in the -80°C freezer, not the "MASTER" stock).

Plate on M9-dipyridyl selective medium (iron-chelated, see reagents).
Wild-type VB-Δsid cannot grow under iron limitation without external
siderophores — rescue of growth on these plates confirms the scavenger
operon is functional.

Incubate at 30°C (NOT 37°C for this strain — VB-Δsid is adapted to subterranean
thermal conditions; the outer membrane starts losing integrity above 33°C).
Growth should appear at 48-72 hrs.

Iron-starved colonies should appear slightly amber under white light (Fe²⁺
accumulation in cytoplasm). Under UV: nothing yet — bioluminescence requires
the overflow valve construct from Protocol V-04.
